Our Story

Building a More Connected Africa

PLES IT SERVICES LIMITED was established with a clear vision to bridge the technology gap across Africa. What started as an IT support and infrastructure company has grown into a multi-service organisation delivering IT reselling, Motorola communication solutions, infrastructure services, TPM, and IOR & logistics across multiple African countries.

Today, we are proud to support businesses, governments and organisations with reliable technology, expert people and a commitment to long-term partnerships.
